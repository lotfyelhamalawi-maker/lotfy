How it works

Three steps, no training experience needed

Dogs hear much higher pitches than people do. The device uses that to get your dog's attention the moment barking starts.

1. Point at your dog

Hold the device facing your dog from a few steps away, with nothing blocking the front grille.

2. Press a button

When barking starts, press one of the three function buttons. The device sends out a short high-pitched sound that dogs hear and most people do not.

3. Reward the quiet

The moment your dog stops, praise it or give a treat. Dogs learn fastest when quiet is rewarded.

Questions

Quick answers

Will I hear the sound?

Most people hear little or nothing, because the pitch is above normal adult hearing. Some people may notice a faint tone.

Does it hurt my dog?

It is meant to be an unpleasant surprise that interrupts barking, not a harmful one. Use short presses and stop if your dog seems distressed.

Does it work on every dog?

Results depend on the dog and on how consistently you reward quiet behaviour. Some dogs respond within days, others need more time.
